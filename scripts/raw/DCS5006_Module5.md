## Section A – Professionalism and Accountability (Questions 1–14)

---

### Question 1

What is professionalism in this context?

- A) Looking professional and being polite only
- B) Wearing a name tag and arriving on time
- C) Agreeing with colleagues at all times
- D) What patients and colleagues can consistently expect from us — reflected in the decisions we make when no one is standing beside us

**Answer: D**

**Explanation:** Professionalism appears in consistent expectations and everyday decisions, not just appearance or manners.

---

### Question 2

What is accountability?

- A) Never making a mistake
- B) Blaming others when things go wrong
- C) Avoiding responsibility as a student
- D) Being answerable for the work we accept and for what we do with the information available to us

**Explanation:** Accountability means owning the work and the information — it does not mean a competent professional never makes a mistake.

---

**Explanation:** Owning mistakes honestly is the essence of accountability.

## Section B – Communication Fundamentals and the Closed Loop (Questions 15–30)

---

### Question 15

How is successful communication defined?

- A) When the sender and receiver understand the message in the same way
- B) When the sender speaks loudly
- C) When the message is sent quickly
- D) When the sender talks the most

**Answer: A**

**Explanation:** The practical test is whether the other person received the message we intended.

---

### Question 16

What is the closed loop of communication?

- A) Send → forget → repeat
- B) Write → file → ignore
- C) Speak → leave → hope
- D) Send → receive → confirm, with the receiver reporting back when the call is completed or if they cannot reach someone

**Answer: D**

**Explanation:** Confirmation and report-back close the loop; the task is verified as accepted and acted upon.

---

### Question 17

Which statement about silence is correct?

- A) Silence confirms the message
- B) Silence means agreement
- C) Silence does not prove that an action happened
- D) Silence is the best confirmation

**Answer: C**

**Explanation:** Without confirmation, you cannot know whether the action occurred.

---

### Question 18

You ask a colleague to call cardiology about an urgent concern. What best completes the communication loop?

- A) The colleague confirms the action and reports back
- B) The colleague nods
- C) You assume it was done
- D) You mention it once and leave

**Answer: A**

**Explanation:** Closed-loop communication confirms the task was accepted and the message reached the intended person.

---

### Question 19

Which statement about professional communication is correct?

- A) Professional communication is what you say, how you say it, what you document, what you confirm, and what you escalate
- B) Professional communication is speaking politely only
- C) Professional communication excludes documentation
- D) Professional communication applies only to patients

**Answer: A**

**Explanation:** The definition encompasses speech, manner, documentation, confirmation, and escalation.

---

### Question 20

- A) Only sending is a responsibility
- B) Only receiving is a responsibility
- C) Both sending and receiving are responsibilities — checking the message was understood belongs to the process
- D) Neither is a responsibility

**Explanation:** Professional communication includes sending, receiving, checking understanding, and responding appropriately.

---

### Question 21

- A) A personal text message

- C) A hallway conversation without confirmation

---

### Question 22

- B) Busy departments do not need closed loops
- C) Interruptions and misunderstandings are common, so confirmation is essential

### Question 23

### Question 26

### Question 27

### Question 28

### Question 29

### Question 30
