- B) Any information found in a hospital

---

### Question 16

- B) Any patient's family member
- C) Any member of the public
- D) A hospital or certain healthcare providers with custodial responsibility for health information

**Explanation:** HICs — such as hospitals and certain providers — are legally responsible for personal health information.

---

### Question 17

How should staff and students handle personal health information?

- A) According to personal judgement alone
- B) Through personal accounts and private groups
- C) Within the custodian's authorized processes

---

### Question 18

- B) The professional's duty to protect information
- C) The employer's data storage policy

### Question 19

- B) The patient's right to demand silence
- C) The employer's right to publish records

### Question 20

- B) The purpose must be curiosity
- C) The purpose may be personal interest

**Explanation:** Only a healthcare-provision purpose supports reliance on implied consent in the circle of care.
